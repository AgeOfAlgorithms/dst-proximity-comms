[h2]0.1.3[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]New speech bubbles:[/b] lines now sit in a see-through dark speech bubble, in Pangolin (the round, easy to read font of the Teardown version). Russian is drawn in it too; Chinese and other scripts use the game's own fonts.
[*][b]The bubble's tail points at whoever is talking[/b] and follows them as they move - under the bubble over their head, or toward the screen edge when they're off screen.
[*][b]New settings[/b] (Chat page): [b]Bubble background[/b] (0-100 %, default 60 %) and [b]Bubble style[/b] ("The game's" brings back the old hand-drawn look). Bubble text size works with the new font.
[*][b]Controller Quick chat:[/b] [b]X[/b] opens a folder or adds the line, [b]A[/b] sends and closes, [b]Y[/b] takes the last word back, [b]B[/b] goes back, [b]hold B[/b] cancels.
[/list]
